SurroundSpace Privacy Policy

JudgeKing Gaming
Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy applies to SurroundSpace, our mixed-reality workspace app for Meta Quest. It explains information the app processes, including information used temporarily on your device, what can remain after you close the app, and your choices.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or security reports, contact shawn.simpson@rogers.com.

At a glance

  • SurroundSpace processes tracking and room information to operate and restore your workspace. Layouts, browsing data, notes and connection settings can remain on your headset.
  • Websites receive your web requests and information you submit. Mirroring and remote desktop connect to your chosen devices; an iPhone connection through a Mac uses your own Mac as an intermediate relay.
  • Optional weather and dictation features can send information to external providers. Optional camera casting shares a view with a browser on your local network.
  • The optional Saved details feature stores text you enter in a local unencrypted file. Captured photos and videos are saved to shared storage and may remain after the app's private data is removed.
  • You may request deletion free of charge using the contact above. Removing local app data does not delete information held by websites or other services.

Workspace, movement and room information

SurroundSpace uses headset position and orientation, controller input and supported hand-tracking and gesture input to position the workspace and let you operate its controls. This is processing of device information even when used only to respond to you in real time.

Saved workspaces can include named layouts, your primary-room choice, window sizes and positions, docking settings, drawings, and references to placed 3D models and their positions. To recognize a physical room, the app can save Quest room, floor and wall identifiers and relative positions and orientations using Meta's scene information. The reviewed room-storage implementation stores these workspace records on the headset and does not include an app upload of the room scan or layout.

Displaying standard Meta passthrough is different from giving an app access to raw camera images. Camera capture and casting are separate features that may process imagery of your surroundings. Consider other people and private information visible in your room before using them.

Web browsing and website accounts

The embedded browser connects from your Quest to the websites you open. Those websites and their service providers receive ordinary web requests, including your IP address and information you submit, such as sign-in details, searches or form entries. If you choose to open a page in Meta Quest Browser, SurroundSpace passes the address to that browser. The website's and browser provider's privacy practices apply to their processing.

Normal tabs can retain addresses, bookmarks, cookies, cached content and website storage, including information that keeps you signed in. Browsing history is enabled by default and can be disabled or cleared. A saved room can also remember the last address passed to Quest Browser.

Private-tab addresses are excluded from room saves and browsing history, and private browsing uses separate storage. Cleanup after an interruption can occur on a later launch. Private browsing does not hide your requests from websites, network operators or services you use, and is not a guarantee that every trace disappears immediately.

Saved details

SurroundSpace does not provide an automatic website-password vault. Its optional Saved details feature stores text you explicitly enter in a local, unencrypted file.

Do not use Saved details for passwords, payment information or other sensitive information. Treat it as ordinary saved text, not secure credential storage.

Phone mirroring, remote desktop and pairing

Mirroring and remote-desktop features process screen content and connection information to display your chosen phone or computer. Remote-control functions send your input to that device. Screen content can include messages, notifications, documents and account information. Only connect devices you are authorized to access.

The reviewed implementation connects the Quest to the device you pair with, without a developer-operated server or third-party cloud relay in these paths. When connecting an iPhone through a Mac, your own Mac runs the relay. This describes the app's reviewed implementation, not a network-traffic audit or a guarantee about every service running on your connected devices.

The app can remember device names, addresses and connection choices. Choosing Remember stores a reusable pairing credential protected using Android Keystore. Disconnect a session when you finish, and remove remembered access when you no longer want a device to reconnect.

Smart-home connections

If you set up supported smart-home integrations, SurroundSpace processes the device addresses, commands and connection information needed to communicate with the services you select. It can retain encrypted TV keys or tokens and an optional Home Assistant access token. These credentials permit access to the connected services; revoke them with the relevant service or device when that access is no longer wanted. Their providers' privacy practices also apply.

Notes, attachments, photos and videos

Notes and attachments can persist after you close the app. Photos and videos you explicitly capture are written to the headset's shared Pictures or Movies storage. These files can outlast the app's private data and must be managed separately.

Optional camera casting serves the view from your headset to a browser on the local network. The receiving browser or device processes the shared view. Share only with intended recipients and stop casting when you finish; do not assume that content displayed to someone else cannot be copied or recorded.

Optional weather and dictation

Weather requests send the coordinates of your selected location to the configured weather provider, MET Norway by default, to retrieve forecasts. This is a transfer off the headset even if the location is not your current physical location.

Dictation uses the headset's speech-recognition provider. That provider may process speech online. Use dictation only when you are comfortable with that provider processing what you say; you can type instead.

Diagnostics, Meta and Unity

The reviewed app implementation has no SurroundSpace account system and no app code that uploads usage analytics or crash reports to a server operated by JudgeKing Gaming. Unity Analytics, Ads, Crash Reporting and Performance Reporting are disabled in the reviewed project service settings. A separate Unity Player setting, submitAnalytics, is enabled. These configuration findings are not a measurement of all traffic from a release build, and we do not claim that Unity receives no technical information.

Meta operates the Quest platform and can make platform-generated usage statistics and crash diagnostics available through its developer dashboard, including aggregate crash counts and sampled stack traces. This platform processing is distinct from SurroundSpace's own code. Meta and Unity describe their processing in their privacy information:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy and developer privacy FAQ

Support and third parties

If you contact us, we receive your contact information and the content and attachments you send so we can respond, investigate issues or handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Websites, Meta, Unity, speech-recognition providers, weather providers, connected devices and smart-home services may process information in different countries under their own practices. A third party's policy does not replace this explanation of SurroundSpace's processing. Review the privacy information for the services you choose, particularly before submitting sensitive information.

Retention and your controls

Closing SurroundSpace does not necessarily remove saved records. Browser state, rooms, notes, saved details and remembered connections can persist for later use. Use the app's available controls to manage saved content and browsing history. Disabling history does not itself delete existing records or website-held information.

Removing app-private storage does not remove shared photos or videos, files on connected devices, information held by websites or providers, or any separately retained backup copies. Manage shared media and provider-held information separately. Signing out of websites, stopping remote sessions and revoking pairing or service credentials can limit future access but do not necessarily erase prior records.

Access, correction and deletion

All users may email shawn.simpson@rogers.com to request information about personal data we handle, access or correction, or deletion. Identify SurroundSpace and the information or feature involved. We may request information reasonably needed to verify the request or locate records; do not send passwords.

Deletion requests are free of charge and will be addressed without undue delay. Where information is stored only on your device and we cannot remove it remotely, we will explain how to remove the relevant records. We will tell you if we hold no responsive information. If applicable law requires retention of particular records, we will explain the reason and limit retention accordingly. Requests relating to information controlled independently by a website or another provider should also be directed to that provider.

You can contact us about withdrawing consent and the effect on a feature. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional privacy rights and may raise a concern with your local privacy regulator, including the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security and changes

No device, storage method or connection is completely secure. Keep your headset and connected devices protected and avoid sharing sensitive content with unintended recipients. The specific protections described above, such as Keystore-protected pairing credentials, do not mean every saved record or connection has the same protection.

We will update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide notice and obtain consent before applying new uses or disclosures to personal information.
